1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & CORE PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"It's A Simple Job" is a direct peer-to-peer short-haul courier and express
delivery web application operating across South Brisbane to the Gold Coast and
Coolangatta in 100% Australian Dollars ($ AUD).

Unlike traditional delivery platforms that take a 20%–30% commission from every
job and mark up customer prices, "It's A Simple Job" connects Clients directly
with independent Motor Vehicle Courier Drivers and Bicycle Couriers:
- Clients pay 100% of the delivery fare directly to their chosen courier via
  the courier's registered PayID (Mobile, Email, or ABN) in $ AUD.
- Couriers set their own custom per-kilometre ($/km), Same-Day, and Urgent
  2-Hour Express rates, plus their own maximum package weight (kg) and
  dimensions (Height × Width × Length in cm).
- Instead of percentage commissions, approved couriers use whole-number
  Platform Tokens ($1 AUD per token default) deducted by road-distance bracket
  when receiving a customer booking (or 0 tokens during a SuperAdmin 1-Hour
  Free Mode window).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When visitors first enter the web app, a centered responsive modal dialog
(`#welcome-intro-popup`) titled "About It's a Simple Job" introduces the three

   - Get paid 100% directly by clients straight to your PayID.

2. 🚲 Bicycle Couriers
   - Deliver letters, documents, and food up to 8 kg by bike or e-bike.
   - Set your own rates with automatic protection against oversized cargo.
   - Take fast, short-haul express deliveries on your own schedule.
   - Get paid 100% directly by clients straight to your PayID.

3. 📦 Clients
   - Book reliable point-to-point deliveries between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM.
   - View available couriers on the live map and pick who delivers.
   - Search directly for your preferred driver or rider by their @username.
   - Pay couriers 100% directly via PayID with zero platform markups.

3. USER ROLES, ONBOARDING WORKFLOW & RBAC PERMISSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The platform enforces a strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) lifecycle:

A. Logged-Out Visitor
   - Sees "Register / Login" on the top-right Google button.
   - Can browse the "Closest Drivers" live GPS radar map, inspect the "Booking
     Form", and search drivers by `@username` or suburb.
   - Cannot submit a booking or apply as a driver until they sign in with
     Google and complete their Community Supporter registration first.
   - Signing out clears all active session keys so a logged-out visitor is
     never falsely shown as logged in.

B. Community Supporter / Client (`role: 'supporter'`)
   - Every first-time user who signs in with Google must register as a
     Community Supporter first by saving their Full Name, Email, Mobile Phone,
     and Suburb/Address in the Supporter Panel.
   - Once registered as a Supporter:
     * Their contact details automatically sync into Step 3 of the Booking Form
       so they can verify or edit their details before selecting a driver.
     * They can view their past and active deliveries in "My Bookings History"
       and exchange 2-way messages with their assigned courier.
     * They can apply to become a Courier Driver via the "Register as a Driver"
       form (located directly under "Buy Tokens" in the Supporter Panel or via
       the footer link). While their driver application is pending, they remain
       a Community Supporter until approved by SuperAdmin.

C. Approved Courier Driver / Bicycle Courier (`role: 'driver'`)
   - Once SuperAdmin approves a Supporter's driver application in the Super
     Admin Panel, the user gains access to the Driver Admin Panel:
     * Toggle their live map profile ON or OFF at any time.
     * Choose and update their unique `@username` handle (e.g., `@callumevans`).
     * Auto-geolocate their live GPS position so they rank accurately by
       distance to the client's pickup address.
     * Configure Motor Vehicle Courier mode, Bicycle Courier mode, or BOTH,
       with independent $/km, Same-Day, and Urgent rates and cargo dimension/
       weight limits.
     * Top up their Driver Token Wallet via Stripe Checkout in AUD.
     * View incoming customer orders, verify direct PayID payments, update job
       dispatch statuses, and message clients.
   - Privacy Guard: A driver can only view and edit their own driver profile;
     driver licence numbers are never exposed publicly and are visible only to
     SuperAdmin.

D. SuperAdmin (`role: 'superadmin'`)
   - Authorized emails: `support@aifoundation.net.au` and
     `davidrobertson.info@gmail.com`.
   - SuperAdmin is always unrestricted across the entire application and has a
     "View Site As Role" switcher (SuperAdmin / Driver / Supporter) inside the
     Google profile menu.
   - Exclusive SuperAdmin capabilities in the Super Admin Panel:
     * Approve individual or all pending driver applications (`Approve All
       Pending Drivers`) so vetted drivers go live on customer maps.
     * Revoke or delete driver profiles with a two-step confirmation guard.
     * Set the AUD price per token and the 4 distance-bracket token charges
       (0–20 km, 21–50 km, 51–100 km, 101+ km).
     * Activate the "1-Hour Free Driver Mode" countdown (0 tokens deducted per
       booking during the active promotional hour).
     * Credit or adjust token balances for any user or driver.

5. PRICING, DIRECT PAYID SETTLEMENT & INTEGER TOKEN ECONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 100% Australian Dollars (AUD) & Whole-Number Rounding:
   - All quotes, rates, token packs, and Stripe charges are in $ AUD.
   - Quotes are calculated from real OSRM road distance (km) between pickup and
     delivery addresses:
     `Total Quote ($ AUD) = ceil(Road Distance km × Driver $/km Rate + Speed Fee)`

2. Direct Customer-to-Driver PayID Settlement:
   - When a client confirms a booking with a live approved driver, the app
     generates a Booking Reference and displays the driver's verified PayID
     (Phone, Email, or ABN) and account name so the client pays the driver
     100% directly.

3. Distance-Bracket Driver Token Deduction:
   - When a booking is placed, integer tokens are deducted from the driver's
     Token Wallet according to the road distance of the delivery:
     * 0 – 20 km:   Default 2 Tokens
     * 21 – 50 km:  Default 5 Tokens
     * 51 – 100 km: Default 10 Tokens
     * 101+ km:     Default 15 Tokens
   - SuperAdmin can customize these brackets in real time or activate 1-Hour
     Free Driver Mode (0 tokens deducted).
   - Token purchases via `/api/stripe-checkout` enforce Australian GST (10% GST
     added on ex-GST base price in integer cents for Stripe Checkout).
