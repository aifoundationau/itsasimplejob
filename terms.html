These terms apply when you use It's A Simple Job to book short-haul couriers, to buy tokens, or to register as a supporter or driver. By using the platform you agree to these terms.

2. How our courier network works

It's A Simple Job operates a peer-to-peer courier network connecting South Brisbane to Coolangatta in 100% Australian Dollars (AUD).

Supporters & verified drivers

People first register as a Community Supporter. Behind the private Supporter Panel, verified members can apply and register as an authorized courier driver with licence details, vehicle type, PayID, and custom rates.

9:00 AM – 4:00 PM pickup window

Customers select a pickup window between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM, specifying package weight (kg) and dimensions (H × W × L cm). Each job is recorded directly into the driver console with complete customer details.

Direct driver PayID settlement

Customers transfer funds directly to the driver's registered PayID in AUD. Customers never pay the web app platform for courier jobs.

3. Tokens

What tokens are

Tokens are how you pay on It's A Simple Job. You exchange money for tokens, then use those tokens to pay for the services and products you book on the platform.

Why we use tokens

Tokens give you more flexible payment options. A job often involves more than one service provider, for example a tradie and a courier, or a crew of several workers. With tokens, a single payment can be distributed to multiple service providers: you pay once, and each provider receives their share for the work they do.

What a token costs

  • One token costs A$1 (one Australian Dollar) unless we show you a different price.
  • The price that applies is the one shown in your Token Wallet when you buy.
  • Tokens are bought and charged in Australian Dollars, wherever you are.

How services and products are priced in tokens

  • We set the token amount for every service and product sold on the platform, such as hourly trade rates, callout fees, courier distance charges and token packs.
  • Where a service provider has set their own rate, for example their hourly rate, we convert it to tokens at the token price.
  • You see the price in tokens and in money before you book.

Whole tokens only

Tokens are always whole numbers. We never use parts of a token. Any calculation that would give a part of a token is rounded up to the next whole token.

Buying and using tokens

  • You buy tokens in your Token Wallet. Card payments are processed securely by Stripe.
  • Tokens are added to your wallet once the payment is confirmed.
  • Your wallet shows the tokens you have available and any tokens held for jobs you have booked.
  • Tokens can only be used to pay for services and products on It's A Simple Job.

Messages

  • Signed-in users can send messages to each other from the Messages panel on their Admin Page.
  • Messages to the superadmin are always free. Replies from the superadmin are free too.
  • A message to another user costs the number of tokens shown before you send it. The tokens are taken from your Token Wallet when the message is sent.

4. Currency, pricing and rounding

Australian Dollars for everything

All prices, quotes, invoices and payments on It's A Simple Job are in Australian Dollars (AUD). This applies to every customer, wherever they are located.

Whole dollars only, rounded up

We only use whole numbers. Prices, fees, GST, totals, tokens, billable hours and billable distances are all whole numbers. Wherever a calculation would produce a fraction, we round it up to the next whole number. The only exception is the USD conversion described below, which is shown to the cent.

Example: a job estimated at 1½ hours is billed as 2 hours. A courier run of 78.4 km is charged as 79 km. GST of $24.60 is charged as $25.

Your region

We use geolocation (based on your internet connection, or your device's GPS if you allow it) to set your default region. You can change your region at any time in the regional settings at the bottom of the home page.

  • Australia and New Zealand: prices are shown in Australian Dollars.
  • All other countries: prices are also shown as an approximate conversion to US Dollars (USD).

USD conversions are for information only

  • USD amounts are converted from AUD using a currency converter at a daily exchange rate. They show 2 decimal places at most, rounded up to the next cent (for example, $98 AUD may show as ≈ $68.85 USD).
  • They are a guide only. You are always invoiced and charged in Australian Dollars.
  • Your bank or card provider converts the AUD charge into your own currency at its own rate and may charge its own fees. The amount on your statement may therefore differ from the USD amount we showed you.

5. Invoices and GST

  • All invoices are in Australian Dollars (AUD).
  • Each booking generates a tax invoice that lists the services, the GST and the total in whole Australian Dollars, with the matching number of tokens alongside.
  • Australian Goods and Services Tax (GST) at 10% is added to the services on your invoice and shown as a separate line. The invoice total includes GST.

6. Refunds

Refund requests must be made in writing, by sending a message to our superadmin (the administrator who manages payments on It's A Simple Job). Use the Messages panel on your Admin Page, or email support@aifoundation.net.au. Messages to the superadmin are free: they never cost tokens or any fee.

  • This applies to refunds of token purchases and of payments for jobs.
  • So we can find your payment, please include your docket number (for example IASJ-123456) or the date and amount of your token purchase, your Customer Reference Number (CRN) if you have one, and the reason for your request.
  • Refunds are calculated and paid in Australian Dollars.
