Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Quick answers about our 24/7, 365 days a year registration, CRN & SPN references, pricing, and distance calculations.

Your Customer Reference Number (CRN) is your permanent digital identity across our national 24/7, 365 days a year service platform (e.g. CRN-849102). You can generate one instantly via the Settings Wheel > For Customers menu or automatically upon completing your first booking. Quoting your CRN unlocks 1-click booking, priority 24/7/365 emergency dispatch, and direct access to your past job history and tax invoices.

Licensed contractors and courier operators can register via Settings Wheel > For Service Providers. Upon registering your trade licence (e.g. QBCC), working hours, pricing, and service radius, an official Service Provider Number (SPN) (e.g. SPN-294810) is immediately issued. This SPN activates your profile in our live dispatch feed and links your insurance verification documents hosted on ImgBB album k4vjCb.

Providers have complete flexibility to configure their transparent pricing models:

  • Hourly Rates: Clearly stated rate per hour for trade work or on-site diagnostic time.
  • Flat Rates: Upfront fixed price for defined standard tasks.
  • Base Call-Out Fee: Standard call-out fee to cover arrival, inspection, and tooling.
  • Distance Charges: Callout or courier freight rate charged per km or mile beyond the included free travel zone (e.g. $1.50/km after 10 km).

Our platform automatically detects your operating region. Australia (AU), New Zealand (NZ), and Canada (CA) default to Kilometres (km). When operating in the United States (US) or United Kingdom (UK), the system automatically converts and quotes in Miles (mi). Providers can also manually switch their preferred measurement unit anytime in their profile settings.

Yes! We operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year — including all nights, weekends, and public holidays. Service providers register their on-call availability, including 24/7, 365 Days a Year Urgent On-Call, Morning (7am–12pm), Afternoon (12pm–5pm), Evening (5pm–10pm), and Overnight (10pm–6am) shifts. Urgent emergency callouts for burst pipes, electrical faults, or medical logistics are routed immediately to active on-call specialists round the clock.

Customers pay the service provider or franchise directly. We do not take the customer's payment. Instead, we charge service providers and franchises a small fee per transaction. Every booking still generates an official Australian Tax Invoice docket (e.g. IASJ-XXXXXX) detailing labour, materials, callout fee, distance travel, and 10% GST, and all of it is available in your own admin panel.

Recruiters and site managers can visit the Recruit tab to filter vetted candidate resumes by keywords (e.g., "Plumber", "Licensed Sparky", "Forklift") and location. You can inspect full CVs, verified licences, police checks, and shift availability, then submit an instant placement request directly through the portal.
