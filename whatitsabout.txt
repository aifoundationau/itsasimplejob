OVERVIEW
--------
"It's A Simple Job" is an Australian Direct-Driver Courier & Express Delivery
platform operating in Australian Dollars ($ AUD) with live OpenStreetMap GPS
routing, a 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM pickup booking window, direct Customer-to-Driver
PayID settlement (100% of the delivery fare goes directly to the driver), and a
flat distance-tier Token system instead of high percentage commissions.

Every new user first registers as a Community Supporter via Google Sign-In.
Supporters can book couriers immediately or apply to become a Driver (which is
reviewed and approved by the SuperAdmin in the Super Admin Panel before going
live on the customer maps).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FROM THE PERSPECTIVE OF A COURIER DRIVER (Motor Vehicle: Van, Ute, Car)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is for you:
- You are an independent motor-vehicle courier driver who sets your own rates,
  chooses your own @username handle (e.g. @callumevans), and keeps 100% of the
  delivery fee paid by the client directly to your PayID.

How it works for a Courier Driver:
1. Register as a Supporter First, Then Apply as a Driver:
   - You sign in with Google ("Register / Login"), complete your Community
     Supporter details first, and then submit the "Register as a Driver" form
     (located under "Buy Tokens" in the Supporter Panel or via the footer link).
   - You provide your Driver Licence details (kept private and visible only to
     SuperAdmin), vehicle description, base suburb/GPS coordinates, PayID
     details (Mobile, Email, or ABN), and your custom @username.
   - Your application enters "Pending SuperAdmin Approval" until the SuperAdmin
     approves you in the Super Admin Panel.

2. Set Your Own Vehicle Rates & Package Limits ($ AUD):
   - You control your own Motor Vehicle pricing and cargo capacity:
     * Per-Kilometre Rate ($/km in AUD, e.g. $2.10/km – $2.80/km)
     * Same-Day Pickup Fee ($ AUD)
     * Urgent 2-Hour Express Fee ($ AUD)
     * Maximum Weight (up to the platform 50 kg limit per item) and Maximum
       Dimensions: Height (cm) x Width (cm) x Length (cm).
   - Note: You can also optionally enable Bicycle Courier mode on your profile
     if you offer both services.

3. Live GPS Map Visibility & Profile ON/OFF Toggle:
   - Once approved by SuperAdmin, you can toggle your Driver Profile ON or OFF
     at any time in your Driver Admin Panel.
   - When turned ON, your live GPS pin, @username, vehicle symbol, and instant
     route quote appear on the client maps ("Book Courier" & "Closest Drivers").

4. Receiving Bookings, Getting Paid 100% via PayID, and Token Tiers:
   - When a client books you for a 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM pickup, you receive an
     instant booking alert, full pickup/dropoff route details, package specs,
     and a direct 2-way message thread with the client.
   - The client pays 100% of your quoted AUD fare directly into your PayID
     account before/at dispatch.
   - Instead of paying 20%–30% commission to a platform, you only use integer
     Platform Tokens per completed order based on road distance:
     * 0 – 20 km:    2 Tokens
     * 21 – 50 km:   4 Tokens
     * 51 – 100 km:  7 Tokens
     * 101+ km:     10 Tokens
     (During a SuperAdmin "1-Hour Free Mode", 0 Tokens are deducted.)
   - You can top up tokens anytime via Stripe Checkout in AUD ($1.00 AUD per
     token ex-GST + 10% GST for Australian customers).

2. FROM THE PERSPECTIVE OF A BICYCLE COURIER (Bicycle / E-Bike / Cargo Bike)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is for you:
- You are a fast, eco-friendly inner-city or coastal express rider specializing
  strictly in light, compact deliveries — specifically Letters, Documents, and
  Food/Takeaway parcels.

How it works for a Bicycle Courier:
1. Dedicated Bicycle Mode & Symbol Badge:
   - When applying in "Register as a Driver" (after registering as a Supporter
     first and waiting for SuperAdmin approval), you tick the "Bicycle Courier
     (Letters & Food Only)" mode (either as a standalone Bicycle Courier or
     alongside a Motor Vehicle).
   - Your profile displays the Bicycle symbol badge and your custom @username
     so clients can filter or switch your card specifically to Bicycle mode.

2. Strict Weight & Size Protection (Automatic Filtering):
   - Bicycle couriers are protected from heavy or bulky cargo:
     * Allowed Package Types: Letters / Documents and Food / Takeaway only.
     * Maximum Weight: Capped at 8 kg (default) and never above lightweight
       limits.
     * Compact Dimensions: You set your own max Height x Width x Length (cm),
       such as 35 x 30 x 40 cm (pannier/backpack/cargo-box size).
   - If a client enters a heavy parcel or hardware item that exceeds your
     bicycle weight/dimension limits or is not eligible for bicycle delivery,
     the platform automatically filters out or disables Bicycle mode for that
     quote so you are only matched with jobs you can safely carry.

3. Custom Bicycle Rates & 100% Direct PayID Earnings:
   - You set your own Bicycle $/km rate (e.g. $1.50/km – $1.80/km), Bicycle
     Same-Day fee ($ AUD), and Bicycle Urgent Express fee ($ AUD).
   - Just like vehicle drivers, clients pay 100% of your delivery fee directly
     to your PayID, and you use the same flat distance-tier Tokens (typically
     the 0–20 km short-haul tier = 2 Tokens) with zero percentage commission
     taken from your delivery pay.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FROM THE PERSPECTIVE OF A CLIENT (Community Supporter / Customer)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is for you:
- You are a local resident, professional, or business looking for transparent,
  reliable, point-to-point courier delivery between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM where
  you can see the exact road distance, compare nearby drivers, search for your
  preferred driver by @username, and pay your chosen driver directly via PayID.

How it works for a Client:
1. Register as a Community Supporter First:
   - First-time visitors click "Register / Login" with Google and complete their
     Community Supporter profile once (Full Name, Email, Mobile Phone, and
     Address/Suburb).
   - Your contact details automatically sync into the Booking Form so you never
     have to re-type them on future bookings.

2. Step-by-Step "Booking Form" (Collapsed & Easy to Use on Mobile & Desktop):
   - Inside the "Booking Form" box, you check or update 3 simple collapsible
     sections:
     * Step 1: Route & 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM Pickup Window (Pickup origin, delivery
       destination, pickup date, time between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM, and speed
       tier: Same-Day or Urgent 2-Hour Express).
     * Step 2: Package Content Details, Size (H x W x L cm) & Weight (kg) —
       choose Letters, Food, Parcels, or General Courier (up to 50 kg max per
       single item).
     * Step 3: Customer Contact Details (pre-filled from your Supporter profile,
       editable anytime if booking for another pickup contact).

3. Live Map Routing, @username Search & Choosing Your Driver:
   - The OpenStreetMap route map calculates the exact road distance (km) and
     drive time between your pickup and dropoff addresses.
   - Available drivers are ranked by proximity ("Closest Drivers") and filtered
     automatically so only drivers whose vehicle/bicycle can fit your package's
     weight and H x W x L dimensions are selectable.
   - You can also use the Top Navbar Search Bar to search directly for your
     preferred driver by their `@username`, name, or suburb, or switch a driver
     between Motor Vehicle and Bicycle mode using the symbol buttons.
   - Note: Sample demonstration profiles (marked "SAMPLE ONLY") are clearly
     labelled and blocked from being booked so you only ever book real,
     SuperAdmin-approved live drivers.

4. Direct PayID Payment & Two-Way Driver Messaging:
   - Once you select your live driver and confirm the booking, you see the
     driver's verified PayID details and exact total in $ AUD.
   - You pay the driver directly via PayID (no middleman markup on the fare)
     and can message your driver directly inside "My Bookings History" in your
     Supporter Panel to coordinate pickup and delivery.
